Desart in Marthandam

Designed here. Made here.

Marthandam is home. It is where our first drawing board went up, where our factory floor runs six days a week, and where most of our team lives. That proximity changes everything about how a project feels — a site visit is fifteen minutes away, a shutter that needs re-finishing goes back to the workshop the same afternoon, and you can walk in to see your wardrobe being built.

We work across the rubber-belt homes of Kuzhithurai and Palliyadi, the independent houses along the NH-66 stretch, and new builds around Thuckalay and Karungal. Heavy monsoons and year-round humidity here are unforgiving on cheap material, so we specify BWP-grade plywood, sealed edge-banding and moisture-resistant hardware as standard — not as an upgrade.

  • In-house factory in Marthandam — no third-party carpentry, no blame games.
  • Same-day site visits anywhere in the Kuzhithurai–Thuckalay belt.
  • Moisture-resistant materials chosen for high-rainfall Kanyakumari homes.

Interior services in Marthandam

Everything from a single modular kitchen to a full turnkey home — designed, manufactured and installed by the same team.

Modular Kitchens

Marine-ply carcasses, soft-close hardware and layouts planned around the way Kanyakumari kitchens actually cook — heavy vessels, wet grinding and daily masala prep.

Wardrobes & Storage

Sliding, hinged or walk-in — built floor-to-ceiling to your wall, with internal layouts planned around sarees, suitcases and the storage every family underestimates.

Living & TV Units

Feature walls, concealed wiring, veneer and fluted panelling — the first room your guests see, treated with the detail it deserves.

Full Home Interiors

A single turnkey scope covering every room, with one project manager, one schedule and one bill — from empty shell to move-in ready.

False Ceiling & Lighting

Layered lighting plans, cove detailing and profile lights — designed to make modest ceiling heights feel taller and evenings feel warmer.

3D Design & VR Walkthrough

Photoreal renders and a 360° walkthrough of your own home before a single sheet is cut — so nothing on site is a surprise.
